# Third-party brand assets

Inboxwell uses the following unmodified sign-in assets solely to identify the
authentication provider on the **Add an account** screen:

- Microsoft sign-in symbol, downloaded from the Microsoft identity platform
  branding guidance: https://learn.microsoft.com/entra/identity-platform/howto-add-branding-in-apps
- Google sign-in icon, from Google's pre-approved Sign in with Google asset
  bundle: https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines
